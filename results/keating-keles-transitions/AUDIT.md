Three potential specializations received particular attention.

- Han, Masuda, Singh and Thiel maximize finite-product entries. For their generators the product has trace $2+uv$ with positive integers $u,v$. Our generator product has trace $2+h^2$, strictly between two and three throughout $0<h<1$. Simultaneous similarity preserves trace. [Theorem 1](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/u31/u31.pdf#page=10)
- Gao's extension of a theorem of Jørgensen and Smith applies to the very same real generators and finite lengths. Its objective is trace with prescribed letter counts. With $R=I+E_{12}/2$ and $L=R^T$, the cyclic shifts $RLR$ and $RRL$ each have trace $5/2$. Their largest entries are $5/4$ and $3/2$, respectively. Thus the trace equality classes cannot supply the required entry classification by direct substitution. [Gao, Theorem 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.07340v2#page=6)
- Kan allows the number of continuant digits to vary at fixed sum, but requires positive integer digits. A shear run of length $a$ gives the digit $ha$ here. Rescaling changes the continuant even at two digits, since $K(hx,hy)=1+h^2xy$. [Kan, corrected Theorems 3.4–3.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.03789#page=4)
